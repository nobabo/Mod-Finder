# Game default themes

The **Game default / 게임 기본** preference follows the selected game. Minecraft uses a muted mint background (`#c6ddcf`); Stardew Valley uses a muted cream background (`#e8d9b5`). Each game has five locally bundled publisher screenshots mixed into the canvas at 18%. They crossfade every 20 seconds in a shuffled order, pausing while a dialog is open or the page is hidden. The first photo is shuffled too; optional session storage avoids repeating the last photo immediately after a reload. Each completed round is reshuffled without repeating its final photo at the boundary. Crimson uses the same ordering policy with its original screenshot collection. Reduced motion keeps the current image still. Game default and Crimson are the only theme choices in settings.

The theme preference is saved as `modfinder-theme`. Existing Crimson choices migrate from `modfinder-accent`; removed color themes fall back to Game default. The background color picker applies a six-digit hex color immediately and saves it separately as `modfinder-background-color`, across games and themes. Reset color removes that override and restores the selected theme’s background. The text, controls, and glass highlights adapt to light or dark backgrounds. Custom backgrounds retain the photographs and real refraction renderer.

Steve and the Junimo are the respective central representatives above the home search field, aligned with its center and the brand logo. Companion pairs cluster around the ranking's lower corners. Compact layouts bring both pairs just below those corners, preserving the filter button's position and keeping every label clear. The representative is clipped above the transparent search field so no artwork shows through its input. Characters are decorative, hidden from assistive technology, and ignore pointer events.

Both game and color themes share the `Atmosphere` WebGL refraction renderer. The light shader mixes each photo into the pastel canvas before sampling the same scene through search, ranking and option-card lenses; it does not add opaque white panel fills. The default Crimson shader preserves its original photo/aurora treatment; a custom Crimson background uses the chosen canvas instead. CSS blur and gradient rims remain a fallback when WebGL is unavailable. Particle effects remain limited to color themes.

Mouse motion eases the background by at most 7px horizontally and 5px vertically. The same smoothed pointer drives small depth-dependent character movements and a gentle 3D brand tilt. Home-logo hover adds a small scale response. Touch does not drive the mouse effects; hidden pages and dialogs pause the scene, and reduced motion disables parallax and graphic transforms.

## Background collection

The five sources per game, dimensions, and local paths are recorded in `src/shared/data/game-theme-backgrounds.json`; assets are in `src/web/public/theme-backgrounds/`. Minecraft scenes come from the [official introduction](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/about-minecraft) and [store gallery](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/store/minecraft-java-bedrock-edition-pc): biomes, building, survival, a portal, and exploration. Stardew Valley scenes come from its [official homepage gallery](https://www.stardewvalley.net/): a summer farm, the night market, an autumn multiplayer farm, the mines, and spring scenery. The original color themes' separate screenshot catalog is unchanged.
